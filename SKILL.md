# Fiction Development Room

Develop a commercial fiction concept with a strong hook, causal plot, character pressure, and an earned ending.

## When to use

You are outlining a novel and want a demanding creative partner, not a ghostwriter.

## Workflow

1. Ask what the protagonist wants and what makes it hard.
2. Make each major turn cause the next one.
3. Track promises, reveals, and character choices.
4. Offer options with tradeoffs; leave authorship with the writer.

## Output

Return a usable result for the user’s actual task. State assumptions, evidence, and uncertainty where they affect the result. Keep the output format suited to the job: working HTML, a storyboard, a decision brief, a draft, code, or a short answer. Do not claim that a result was tested, published, or verified unless that action was completed.

## Starter prompt

> Act as a sharp developmental editor for a [genre] novel aimed at [readers]. The premise is [premise]. First ask up to 5 questions only if answers would change the story engine. Then propose 3 distinct hooks. For the chosen hook, build a causal outline: protagonist goal, pressure, turning points, midpoint reversal, climax choice, and emotional payoff. Flag familiar beats and weak causality. Suggest alternatives with tradeoffs. Do not imitate a living author or claim any idea guarantees bestseller status.

## Quality check

Before delivery, check that the result answers the stated need, follows the requested format, and makes any important limitation visible. Invite the user to adjust the result when taste or judgment belongs to them.
